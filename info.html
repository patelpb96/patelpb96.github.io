Patent Claim Analysis

Claim 16 - Photonics Integrated Package Assembly

Claim 16 Teachings

Winzer teaches a method for assembling a photonics integrated package (Fig 2, Fig 5-7, Data processing system 200 assembly inherently requires method steps), the method comprising:

  • integrating a processor on a substrate
    electronic processor integrated circuit 240 mounted on package substrate 230; Col. 8, lines 35-45
  • electronically connecting the processor to an application-specific integrated circuit ("ASIC") comprising a serializer/deserializer ("SerDes") transceiver
    processor integrated circuit 240 can include monolithically embedded therein an array of serializers/deserializers 247 coupled to traces 231; Col. 8, lines 25-35
  • integrating a photonics direct drive engine ("PDDE") on the substrate
    integrated optical communication device 210 on package substrate 230
    wherein integrating the PDDE comprises:
    • integrating the PIC on the substrate
      photonic integrated circuit 214 disposed on substrate
    • bonding part of the EIC over the PIC
      electronic communication integrated circuit 215 is bonded to photonic integrated circuit 214 via electrical coupling at surfaces 215_1 and 214_2, using solder bumps or copper pillars; Col. 10, lines 1-10; Figs. 2-4

Gaps in Winzer

Winzer does not explicitly teach:
  • an interface selection switch coupled to the SerDes

    (however, switches are well known in the art and an obvious design choice in a serializing/deserializing apparatus, as the plurality of paths necessitate switching functionality)

  • wherein the interface selection switch is configurable in a first mode to direct parallel communication signals to the SerDes transceiver
  • wherein the interface selection switch is configurable in a second mode to bypass the SerDes transceiver for direct transmission of serialized communication signals

Additional References

Huff - SerDes Pass-Through Mode

teaches a method of operating a device with a SerDes pass-through mode (Title), wherein:

  • a configurable switch is used
    Figure 1, path selector 116 is coupled to SerDes devices 110, 112, and 114, and is recognized as a configurable switch for routing signals throughout the device
  • the interface selection switch routes parallel electrical signals from the processor to the SerDes transceiver in a first configurable mode of operation and a serialized electrical signal from the processor around the SerDes transceiver in a second configurable mode of operation
    paragraph 29 explains how the path selector 116 is initially in a first position, and couples parallel port 177 for bi-directional deserialized data signals; the SerDes pass-through mode is enabled by switching to a second switch position

Huff does not explicitly teach a PDDE (photonics driver with an EIC and PIC), however, such architectures are commonplace in the art.

Obviousness Conclusion

Before the effective filing date of the claimed invention, one of ordinary skill in the art would have found it obvious to modify the method described in Winzer under the teachings of Huff and Bai to include a SerDes with a configurable switch and routing architecture, wherein an EIC and PIC are disposed on a substrate forming a PDDE, and the PIC carries the SerDes switch components for multimode operation.

This may be accomplished using components and techniques known to a skilled artisan, and would predictably result in a method of assembling a device that is capable of SerDes transmission to operate in multiple modes for fast and variable computation/signal transmission.

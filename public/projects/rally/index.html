Rally

A paddle-and-ball game with nothing painted: every piece is an SVG element, moved by a simulation that never looks at the clock. Move your paddle as you hit the ball to put spin on it.

tick 0 · state 0x00000000 W S or pointer · Space start/pause · . step · R reset

State

The whole game is 22 numbers. A pure step function advances them one sixtieth of a second at a time; it never reads the clock, the page or a random number.

Picture

Each frame rewrites the attributes of about twenty SVG elements from those numbers. The trail, the ripple and the screen shake are all derived, so the picture stays sharp at any size.

Replay

Since the step is deterministic, a game is fully described by its inputs. The replay feeds your key presses back in from tick zero and has to land on the same state hash.
